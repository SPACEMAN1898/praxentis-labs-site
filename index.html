01 — Scope

Tools that live inside the clinic.

Labs is the product-development division of Praxentis. Everything is designed in-house, built on a modern stack, and shaped by daily use in real allied-health practices.

/software

Clinical software

Purpose-built applications for clinic teams — intake, outcomes, communication and workflow — designed around how a practice actually runs.

/tools

Internal tools

Small, sharp utilities that remove friction from the working day: reminders, form handling, decision support, desk-side helpers.

/integrations

PMS integrations

Extensions that sit on top of the practice-management systems clinics already use — no migration, no retraining, no lock-in.

/dashboards

Dashboards

Clear operational views over clinic data — schedule, recall, outcomes — so owners can see the practice at a glance.

/custom

Custom clinic software

Bespoke builds for individual practices: one problem, one tool, fitted to the clinic's own workflow and branding.

/education

Clinical education

Study software built from inside the profession — including browser-native 3D anatomy tools for chiropractic students in Adelaide.

Products in the field.

ClinikChat

End-to-end encrypted clinic team chat that lives inside your PMS. Team chat your server can't read — even Praxentis can't decrypt it.

For Australian allied-health clinic teams — practitioners and reception. Free during public beta; planned Solo tier free up to 3 seats, Clinic from $8 AUD per seat per month.

CliniTrack Suite

The clinical workflow for spine. Intake, safety screening, physical exam and outcomes — lumbar and cervical — in one tablet, integrated with Cliniko for schedule and patient data.

Piloting at The Adelaide Disc Center (Park Holme SA) and a Sydney partner clinic. White-label per clinic; includes CliniKiosk, a front-desk kiosk build of the same suite.

— PRAXENTIS OPERATING PRINCIPLE
  • Every product is piloted in real clinics — one in Adelaide, one in Sydney — before release.
  • Designed in-house, validated against published literature, hardened to AHPRA and the Australian Privacy Principles.
  • Australian data residency as standard: Fly.io Sydney and AWS ap-southeast-2. Patient data never leaves the country.
  • One operator, end to end — the person who writes the code answers the email.
